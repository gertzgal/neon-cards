# Neon Blackjack: look-and-feel brief

## The vibe
A late-night neon casino seen through an old CRT monitor. It's dark, glowing, and a little retro. Balatro's juice applied to straight blackjack.

## Art
- Chunky pixel art, with every sprite drawn on a fixed pixel grid and scaled up by whole numbers so it stays crisp.
- A mild CRT pass over everything: light scanlines, a soft glow on bright colors, and a slight vignette. No heavy screen warp.
- Cards sway gently while they sit in your hand, and tilt toward the pointer on desktop.

## Palette
- The table is near-black (around #0b0b12) with a faint pink and cyan grid glow at the edges.
- Hot pink (around #ff2e88) is for the player, wins, and the main buttons.
- Cyan (around #2ef2ff) is for the dealer, info, and secondary buttons.
- Busts get a red-pink flash, and blackjacks get a gold-white flash on top of the neon.

## Cards
- Dark faces with neon-outlined pips and ranks. Hearts and diamonds are pink, and spades and clubs are cyan.
- Oversized corner ranks so a hand reads at a glance on a phone.
- Court cards are simple pixel silhouettes in neon outline.
- The back is a pink and cyan pixel pattern with the game's logo.

## Chips
- Chunky pixel coins with neon rims, one color per value (5, 25, 100, 500).
- Chips stack with a satisfying clink and slide into the pot when you bet.

## Game feel
- Dealing is snappy: each card flies in with a little squash on landing, and flips with a quick squash-and-stretch.
- A bust gives a small screen shake and a red-pink flash on the hand.
- A normal win gets a payout number pop and chips sliding to you.
- A blackjack gets a big number pop, a chip burst, and a gold flash. That's the biggest moment in the game.
- Every effect is quick, so you can play hand after hand without waiting.

## Sound
- A lo-fi synthwave loop plays underneath.
- Crunchy chiptune effects cover card snaps, chip clinks, a bust thud, and a rising arpeggio on blackjack.
- There's a mute button plus separate music and effects toggles.

## Layout
- Phone portrait has the dealer on top, your hand in the middle, the bankroll and bet above the buttons, and big thumb-sized Hit, Stand, Double, and Split buttons at the bottom.
- Desktop wide has the table in the center, a left panel with the bankroll, bet, and chips, and a right panel with the hand history and stats.
- Both layouts are designed on their own, not one stretched into the other.

## References (descriptions, no images)
1. Balatro's main game screen, for the CRT glow, card wobble, and number pops.
2. Synthwave and outrun art, for the black background with pink and cyan neon grid lines.
3. Neon signs on a casino strip at night, for how the glow bleeds around the shapes.
4. Game Boy-era card games, for crisp, readable pixel cards at a small size.
